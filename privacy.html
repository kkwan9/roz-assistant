Roz privacy policy

Last updated November 10, 2026.

Roz is a private household assistant that one family runs for itself. The only people who can connect a Google account to Roz are the members of that household. This page describes what Roz does with their Google data.

What Roz accesses

With the account owner's permission, Roz reads the owner's Gmail messages and Google Calendar events. Where the owner has separately allowed it, Roz can also add events to the owner's calendar. Roz cannot send, delete or change email.

How the data is used

Roz uses this data only to help the account owner: summarising what arrived, pointing out deadlines and appointments, and preparing reminders and drafts for the owner to review. To do this, Roz passes relevant message and event text to the AI model service the owner has chosen, which processes it to produce Roz's replies.

Where the data is kept

Roz runs on a private server controlled by the account owner. Notes that Roz writes while helping the owner are stored on that server. Sign-in tokens are stored on that server with access restricted to Roz.

Sharing

Google data is not sold, is not used for advertising, and is not shared with anyone outside the household other than the AI model service described above.

Removing access

The account owner can end Roz's access at any time from Google Account permissions, and can delete Roz's stored notes and tokens from their own server.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the owner through github.com/kkwan9.
